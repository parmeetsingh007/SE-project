Board-approved KYC Refresh Policy, version 3.1 (final, approved)

1. Customers rated high risk must have their KYC information updated at least every 2 years, medium risk every 8 years and low risk every 10 years.
2. The bank must notify each customer at least 30 days before the due date by SMS and email, and again at 7 days.
3. Customers may confirm that their details are unchanged through a self-declaration in the mobile app or net banking, without visiting a branch.
4. Where the address has changed, a proof of the new address must be collected and verified.
5. Updated KYC records must be uploaded to the Central KYC Records Registry within 10 days.
6. Accounts must not be frozen for a missing update until two reminders have been sent and 30 days have passed after the due date. Every freeze must be approved by a branch manager and recorded.
7. All KYC records and refresh evidence must be retained for at least 5 years after the relationship ends and be retrievable within 24 hours for an auditor.
8. Customer identifiers must be encrypted at rest and masked on every screen except for authorised compliance staff.
