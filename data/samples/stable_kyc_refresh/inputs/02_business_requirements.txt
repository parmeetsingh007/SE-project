Business requirements document (final), KYC Refresh Programme, owner: Meera Shah (Business Analyst)

The programme covers all 2.4 million existing retail customers over 24 months. Scope, policy and the regulator's schedule are fixed and have been signed off by the steering committee.

BR-1: The system shall calculate each customer's next KYC due date from the risk category.
BR-2: The system shall send reminders at 30 days and 7 days before the due date.
BR-3: The system shall let a customer submit a self-declaration through the mobile app and net banking.
BR-4: The system shall support address-change proof upload and verification by an operations officer within 2 business days.
BR-5: The system shall submit refreshed records to the central KYC registry within 10 days.
BR-6: The system shall record every account freeze together with the approving branch manager.
BR-7: The system shall produce a monthly compliance report of due, completed and overdue refreshes.
The existing core banking and CRM platforms remain unchanged and are accessed through the approved integration layer.
