Interview with Lukas Weber (Head of Sales)

We lose merchants because onboarding takes days. Competitors approve small merchants in under an hour. I want automated approval for low-risk merchants within one hour and a human only for the rest. We release product changes every two weeks and want onboarding to keep up with that pace.

Merchants should upload their company registration and the IDs of the owners from a web portal, and they should be able to see where they are in the process. Sales also needs a dashboard of applications stuck in review.
