Platform engineering notes (Sven Larsen)

- We deploy continuously to production with automated tests and security scans. Onboarding should use the same pipeline.
- Document checks and screening will use external vendors through APIs. Vendor outages must not lose applications.
- Personal data of beneficial owners is processed under GDPR. A data protection impact assessment is needed because we process identity documents at scale.
- The service must handle 500 onboarding applications per hour at peak with 99.9 percent availability.
- Every decision and every access to identity documents must be logged for audit.
