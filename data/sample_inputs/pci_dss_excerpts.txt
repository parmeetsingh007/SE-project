PCI-DSS excerpts (paraphrased summaries for course use, not verbatim standard text)

### PCI-DSS | 8.3.1 | Multi-Factor Authentication for Remote and Administrative Access
All remote network access originating from outside the entity's network into the
cardholder data environment (CDE), and all non-console administrative access to
system components, must use multi-factor authentication (MFA). A single factor
(e.g. password alone) is not sufficient for these access paths.

### PCI-DSS | 8.4.2 | Multi-Factor Authentication for All CDE Access
Multi-factor authentication is required for all access into the CDE, not only
remote access. This extends the MFA requirement to console and internal access
paths as well, effective for assessments after March 31, 2025.

### PCI-DSS | 3.5 | Rendering Stored Primary Account Numbers Unreadable
Primary account numbers (PAN), wherever stored, must be rendered unreadable using
approved methods such as strong one-way hashing, truncation, tokenization, or
strong cryptography with associated key-management processes.

### PCI-DSS | 10.2 | Audit Logging of Authentication and Access Events
Audit logs must capture all individual access to cardholder data, all actions
taken by users with elevated privileges, and all authentication events including
successes and failures, with enough detail to reconstruct what happened.

### PCI-DSS | 6.4.3 | Management of Payment Page Scripts
Scripts loaded and executed on payment pages in the consumer's browser must be
authorized, their integrity confirmed, and an inventory maintained, to prevent
unauthorized script injection into checkout and authentication flows.

### PCI-DSS | 12.10.1 | Incident Response for Suspected Account Takeover
The entity's incident response plan must be tested at least annually and must
include specific procedures for responding to a suspected or confirmed account
takeover or unauthorized authentication bypass affecting cardholder data.
